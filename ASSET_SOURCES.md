Preserve this provenance and obtain an explicit license before reuse beyond the challenge. The upstream source remains authoritative.

The solution ships 22 referenced files (685,083 bytes), including the menu background, water, explosion, cannonball, all nine ship health variants and nine island tiles. The 483 unused pack files were removed from public output after a source/manifest audit and preserved locally in ignored Temp storage. They are still available at the upstream revision above.
